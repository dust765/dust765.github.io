The Project

Why Dust765 Exists

Born from frustration — not with UO itself, but with the drama and gatekeeping in its open-source ecosystem. We forked ClassicUO, stripped the politics, and focused on what matters: a fast, stable, cross-platform client.

What We Built

Features

⚔️
Combat Enhancements

Last target tracking, stun/mortal highlight, swing line indicators, sync position on attack.

🧿
Visual Helpers

Glowing weapons, highlight friends and guild members, health indicator overlay.

🪄
Casting & Spells

Active spell icon on cursor, OnCasting gump, self-cast last target highlight.

🖥️
Modern UI

Custom window title bar with HP/Mana/Stamina bars, borderless window mode.

🌍
Cross-Platform

Native builds for Windows x64, Linux x64, and macOS. All platforms first-class.

🔌
Plugin Compat

Razor Enhanced, UOStealth, ClassicAssist, and more — all work out of the box.
